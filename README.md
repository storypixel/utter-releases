# Utter — releases

Hands-free dictation for macOS. Talk, and your words are typed wherever the
cursor is; say "enter" to send. Everything runs locally on your Mac.

**[Download the latest release](https://github.com/storypixel/utter-releases/releases/latest)**
(Apple Silicon, macOS 14+). Open the DMG, drag Utter to Applications, launch it
— it walks you through setup. Installed apps update themselves automatically.
